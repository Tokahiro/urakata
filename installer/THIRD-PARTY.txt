SudoVDA virtual display driver
==============================

Files: driver\SudoVDA.inf, driver\sudovda.cat, driver\SudoVDA.dll,
driver\sudovda.cer (driver version 1.10.9.289).

Unchanged copies of the files that Apollo 0.4.6 ships in drivers\sudovda
(https://github.com/ClassicOldSong/Apollo/releases/tag/v0.4.6). Driver by
SudoMaker, source: https://github.com/SudoMaker/SudoVDA. Microsoft's sample:
https://github.com/microsoft/Windows-driver-samples
(video/IndirectDisplay/IddSampleDriver).

SudoVDA's README states its license: "MIT and CC0 or Public Domain (for
changes I made, please consult Microsoft for their license), choose the
least restrictive option." The driver is based on Microsoft's
IddSampleDriver from Windows-driver-samples, which is under the Microsoft
Public License (MS-PL), reproduced below. The parts of the driver from
Microsoft's sample are distributed under the MS-PL.

The certificate sudovda.cer (CN=sudovda@su.mk, thumbprint
3C918FC73525AD8B1521B6DB26B71F694277CC49) is SudoMaker's self-signed code
signing certificate. The driver installs only when this certificate is in
the machine's Root and TrustedPublisher stores.
